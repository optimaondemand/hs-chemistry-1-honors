The Question Worth Investigating
This Quarter's Grand Question
“What is matter?”
Semester 1 · Matter has structure at every scale
Keep this question in front of you as you work. In each module, notice how a real observation — a color change, a spectral line, a measured mass — becomes evidence, and how that evidence builds and revises the models we use to explain matter. That movement from phenomenon to evidence to explanation is what it means to think like a chemist.
